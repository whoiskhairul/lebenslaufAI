Professional Profile

 

Write a brief, engaging text about yourself. Provide information about your educational background, work experience, skills, and key achievements relevant to the role you are applying for. Describe in one sentence your most impressive achievement and quantify it. Fill the personal profile with keywords from the job description. Don’t just stuff keywords, but add keywords where they make sense. Try to cover all mandatory requirements within four to five sentences. To make it easier for you, we have created a template – take a look at page 15 in The Secrets To Writing A Germany-Style CV E-book.

Extracurricular Activities

 

·    Adding extracurricular activities is a great way to demonstrate your interests and individuality. They also prove to recruiters that you have a range of valuable skills that you could apply to their vacancy.

·    So, think about including information about your engagement in sports teams, community work, volunteering, or fundraising activities.

·    Keep this section professional and relevant by linking it back to the role you are applying for. If you don’t show how it is relevant (e.g., how your competitive nature could help you thrive in sales), adding your favorite sport to your resume could actually hinder your job application.
